About the Shrine

The Emilio Aguinaldo Shrine is the ancestral residence of the first Philippine President, General Emilio Aguinaldo. It is the historic sanctuary where the Declaration of Philippine Independence was unfurled from the front window on June 12, 1898. The sprawling estate features ingenious secret passageways, antique furniture, ancestral bedrooms, and patriotic emblems intricately carved into indigenous hardwood, enveloped by manicured memorial gardens.
