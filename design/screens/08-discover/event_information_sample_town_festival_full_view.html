About the Festival

The Pahimis Festival is an age-old thanksgiving tradition in Amadeo, affectionately heralded as the "Coffee Capital of the Philippines." Derived from the historic Caviteño ritual where farmers offered their first harvest basket to neighbors and the patron saint, this vibrant fiesta celebrates community bounties, resilience, and the world-renowned Liberica and Robusta beans.

Throughout the three-day carnival, the municipality is transformed into a colorful hub of street revelry. High school and local troupe performers compete in grand street dances adorned with woven bamboo hats, burlap sacks, and authentic coffee garlands. The lively agri-trade fair along Mabini Street welcomes travelers to savor freshly ground roasts, traditional kakanin, and artisanal Cavite products.

Whether you are a coffee aficionado seeking unique single-origin brews, a heritage enthusiast fascinated by highland traditions, or a weekend explorer eager to fill your Cavite Passport, Pahimis delivers warmth, hospitality, and unforgettable cultural pride.
