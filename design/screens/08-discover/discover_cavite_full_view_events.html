Historical and Topographical Riches

From the historic cradle of the Aguinaldo Shrine in Kawit to the temperate volcanic cliffs of the Tagaytay Ridge, and the island bastion of Corregidor guarding Manila Bay, Cavite is an interwoven tapestry of freedom, maritime history, and breathtaking geography.
